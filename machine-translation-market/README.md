Machine translation is becoming increasingly important as enterprises, digital platforms, government agencies, e-commerce companies, healthcare organizations, and content-led businesses seek faster and more scalable multilingual communication. The market is supported by rising cross-border digital activity, improvements in neural machine translation, and growing demand for real-time language translation across business workflows.
